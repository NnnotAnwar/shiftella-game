# Playing Shiftella

You inherit grandma Lida's night café, Café Nocturne, next to Praha-Holešovice station in Prague.
Its doors open at 22:00 and close at 06:00. Keep the light in the window on, keep the regulars
warm, pay off the debt — or let the café go, gently. Nothing here is meant to stress you.

## The rhythm

1. **Day (planning).** The clock waits. On the right is the day card: tonight's story hint,
   money, the next debt payment, who may drop by, train times, how many people are on shift.
   Hire people (**C**), arrange the rozpis in the Shiftella app (**T**), build (**B**), and
   decide who helps with what tonight (**H**, duties). Press **Start the night** (or **Enter**).
2. **21:30 — the preparation half hour.** The night begins with the doors closed (the little
   sign on the door says *Closed*): rain, quiet music, the lights coming up, no guests. Do the
   opening list in peace — light the window, warm up the machine, check the fridge, wipe the
   tables, count the float, chalk tonight's specials, put on a record (Shiftella **T** →
   checklists, or click the little boxes over the furniture — the window lamp, the machine,
   the fridge, a table, the till, the counter, the jukebox). Helpers on tonight's rozpis walk in right
   away (21:31–21:34) and help: they take the opening items of their duties (bar → warm up the
   machine, register → count the float, kitchen → the stock check, the soup on, the milk out,
   the cake in; floor / dishes → wipe the tables) — never one you queued yourself; the
   checklist shows who is on what. With a full crew the café is ready well before 21:59. The
   whole list by 22:30 → *"The café is ready."* (crew mood +2, coziness +2 tonight). The half
   hour's clock runs slower than the night's (about 1.6 real minutes at ×1; people move at the
   usual pace) and **the clock waits at 21:59** until you open: **Open the doors** (**O**)
   beside the coziness meter opens them any time (the clock jumps to 22:00); with the opening
   list done the doors open by themselves at 21:59. No guest ever comes in before. A freight
   train passes by outside at 21:47.
3. **22:00 — the doors open** (a bell, the sign flips to *Open*, toast *Doors open*) and the
   night goes on as always: guests, trains, regulars, Mirek's early visit on night 1 at 22:04.
   **Last call is 05:15**: nobody new comes in after it (walk-ins, train passengers, rushes,
   regulars). From 05:45 whoever is still eating pays and goes, from 05:50 everybody — nobody
   leaves unhappy for it (Cozy or Classic).
4. **06:00 — the doors close** (the sign flips back to *Closed*, toast *Doors closed — time to
   close up*). The café is empty; the closing half hour (06:00–06:30) is as quiet as the
   preparation: count the till, clean the group head, take the trash out, write the handover
   note and **switch off the window light** — the crew does the closing items of their duties
   and goes home when they're done. **Close up** (**O**) ends the night at once (whatever is
   left counts as missed); at 06:29 the clock waits for you, and with the closing list done the
   night ends by itself. The till, the group head and the window light are for closing time
   (or after last call once the café is already empty). Wages still count 22:00–06:00.
5. **Night — you work behind the counter.** Karolina does the café's real work herself:
   greet guests, take their orders, read their wishes, brew, serve, pick up the tips, clear,
   wash up and mop. Helpers (from night 3) only do the duties you give them. Events pop up as
   toasts; some have buttons (they wait while paused, and fall back to a default after a while).
6. **Morning** (when you close up). A short summary of the night: guests, revenue, tips, wages, costs, reputation,
   grandma's notes found — and the night's polaroid developing beside it. Then the next day
   begins (and the game autosaves).

Story mode has 30 nights and exactly two endings — «Light in the Window» and «Last Train».
Sandbox is endless, with a random town from a seed you can share.

### Sandbox modes: Endless or Challenge

The sandbox setup has a **Mode** choice. **Endless** (the default) is the calm sandbox as it
always was: the café runs for as long as you like. **Challenge** gets harder every season
(30 nights) so a run has an end:

* **Money.** From season 2 the rent goes up every season (no ceiling), and so do supply prices
  and wages. Each season has a **goal** — the takings (revenue + tips) to reach over its 30
  nights, bigger every season and for a bigger town. The planning card shows the goal, the
  nights left and your strikes; the morning summary shows how far you've got. Miss the goal
  and it's a **strike**; two strikes in a row and the café closes. Sinking deeper than ten
  nights of rent into the red for three nights in a row closes it too.
* **Rivals.** From season 2 a rival café opens nearby every season (up to three). Each takes
  a share of the walk-ins and wears your reputation down a little — the morning summary says
  how many guests they took. Your strengths win guests back: a cosy hall (coziness level 4+),
  four stars, your own drink on the menu and three regulars who trust you each cut the loss.
* **Nights.** The storyteller grows rougher: more events, more breakdowns, outages, sick calls
  and inspections, less breathing room between them; the appliances wear faster.

The bot announces each season, each rival and each goal met or missed. When the café closes you
get a final page: seasons survived, nights, the best night, guests and money earned, and the
record for that seed (kept in the settings).

Her next things float over her head as small chips, right over her hair (five at most, then
"+3"; the current one with a progress ring). She can hold **eight** things at once. **Her queue**
lives bottom-left, just above the dock: a small strip with the icons and "3/8" — click it (or
**Q**) for the list: every act with an icon and a short label, the current one with its
progress, **↑ / ↓** or a drag to reorder, **×** to drop one, **Clear all** (she finishes what she
is doing). Karolina's crew card (**C**) shows the same whole list. The **ticket rail** at the top shows every open order: who is on it (you, a
helper's initial, or a hollow ring = nobody), and how long they've waited. On Cozy nobody ever
leaves angry: a guest who waits too long gets a glass of water, and later leaves content.

**Where guests order.** Most guests come in, sit down and wait for you (or a helper) to take
their order at the table — the **!** over them. A few order at the bar instead: a quick
coffee, someone alone, a taxi driver or a patrol in a hurry; they stand in the line at the
register. Nobody orders before they are in: a guest still walking in shows a dashed,
dimmed ticket ("still walking in") — a click only reminds you to wait till they sit down or
reach the line. Helpers wait for that too: the **register** helper takes orders at the
register and at the tables, the **serving** helper takes table orders between trays.

**A full café.** Grandma's café has four **bar stools** along the guests' side of the front
counter (left of the register line): when the tables are full, a quick guest — or a group
of four — sits at the bar. A big party takes any free seats close together. When there is
really no seat left, a plain guest (never a regular, never a wish) may **take it to go**: they
queue at the register, wait beside it (a little paper cup over their head), and walk out
with a paper cup — drinks and desserts only, a smaller tip, no dishes. On Classic fewer
bother. Only if the line is long or too many already wait by the register do they go on.
A toast says so now and then, and the morning summary has one line about it ("No free
seat: 3× they took it to go").

**The order card** shows the wish ("something warm, not too sweet — long day") with faint taste
icons, and up to four recipes; matching tastes light up. **Quick** lets the machine do it
(a fine cup, no game); **By hand** plays a short mini-game for a better one. While the card,
a mini-game, the recipe book or the phone is open, the night slows down to a crawl (and
runs at ×1 at most). Plain orders default to Quick, wishes to By hand.

**Mini-games** (8–20 s): milk froth (hold, release in the amber band), latte art (trace the
pattern), pour-over (slow circles in time with the rain), tea (lift the bag at the right colour),
plating (drag the pieces). **Space** holds, arrows / **WASD** steer, **Enter** is done,
**Tab** skips — a skipped cup is still a good cup (Lovely). Settings → **Relaxed mini-games**
widens every sweet spot and waits for a Done button.

**Tips** stay on the table as coins — click them (or let a dishes helper pick them up); at
06:00 the rest goes into the jar anyway. Quick serves one after another build a little amber
rhythm on the tip jar (tips +4 % a step).

**Duties (H)** — helpers only do what you give them: dishes, floor, register, bar, kitchen,
serving; each carries one to three duties. Wishes, regulars, specials and the little moments
are always yours.

**Grandma's jukebox** — Lida's records really change the music (and the mood of the room);
Repeat or Play once. Regulars have favourites. Putting a record on is also part of the
opening ritual.

## The appliances

Everything in the kitchen can be used by hand — one click, sometimes one hold, nothing to
fail. A small amber chip floats over a piece that offers something; hovering shows what a
click does. Helpers with the kitchen duty do the soup, the milk and the cake at 22:00 if you
didn't (with a smaller effect); doing it yourself is quicker and better. None of it is needed
to finish the story.

## Little moments, the cat and the atmosphere

* **Night moments** — a softly glowing orb appears somewhere in the café: the fogged window
  when a train comes, the station cat at the door, a sleeping student, a lost traveller, a
  leak, a blackout… Click the orb (or press **F** for the nearest one) and a small card shows
  two or three choices. Pick one and Karolina walks over to do it (her chip over her head shows
  the moment's icon). Nothing bad happens if you let one pass. A lost traveller asks which
  train: the right one is highlighted.
* **The station cat** — feed it and scratch its ears night after night: it moves from the
  doormat to the radiator, then to the window seat, then follows you around. At the end it
  gets a name.
* **Atmosphere** — the candle under the clock is the hall's coziness (Bare → Like home).
  Hover it to see what it's made of; the weakest part glows in the café (dark seats, dirty
  tables, a silent jukebox). Lamps, rugs, plants, music, a clean floor and the theme raise it;
  cosy halls make guests happier, more patient and more generous.
* **Rituals** — opening items (light the window, warm up the machine, check the stock, wipe the
  tables, count the float, the specials board, a record) and closing items (dishes, clean the
  machine, cash up, the fridge, the handover note, the window light off when you close up) show a small checkbox
  chip on their furniture. Click the chip — or tick them in the app — and Karolina does them;
  helpers do the ones of their duty. A whole list done gives the night a little glow
  ("The café is ready.", "Closed properly.").
* **Hands-on rituals** — when Karolina herself heads for one of those items and you are
  watching at ×1, a small card opens over the piece with its own little gesture (2–5 seconds):

  | item | gesture (mouse · keyboard) |
  |---|---|
  | light the window / the light off at six | pull the lamp's cord down · hold ↓ or Space |
  | warm up the machine | hold until the pressure needle sits in the green, let go · Space |
  | check the stock / the fridge check | tick off the three things on the shelf, the glowing one first · Space |
  | wipe the tables | three cloth strokes across the table · ← → in turn |
  | count the float | tap coins and notes into the tray until it makes tonight's sum (too much? click the tray to take one back) · ← → choose, Space adds, ↓ takes back |
  | the specials board | two chalk strokes · ← → |
  | put a record on | click as the swinging arm passes the green lead-in groove · Space |
  | the dishes | sponge strokes over a plate · ← → |
  | clean the group head | brush strokes · ← → |
  | cash up | each coin to its stack (1 · 5 · 10) · ← ↓ → |
  | the handover note | pick one of the night's lines to write (it becomes the first note) · ↑ ↓ Space |
  | milk out · cake in · trash out (the appliances) | drag the bottle / the tray / the bag along · hold → or Space |
  | soup on | stir once around the pot · hold Space |

  It is never needed: leave it alone and she finishes the item herself exactly as before; at
  ×2 / ×3, under the autoplayer, while another card or a panel is open, or when a helper does
  the item, no card opens at all. Once you touch the gesture she waits for it at the piece (for
  a while at most); finishing it finishes the item at once — usually a little sooner. **Esc**
  or a right-click on the card lets her finish it herself. Pausing pauses it. Mistakes never
  fail anything (a coin too many, the needle let go too early), they only mean *not perfect*;
  a perfect gesture floats a small *Perfect*, and three perfect ones in a list make **a perfect
  opening** (+1 coziness tonight) or **a perfect close** (crew mood +1.5). The first time each
  gesture shows, a one-line hint sits under it (remembered in the settings, shown once).
  *Relaxed mini-games* widen the needle's green and the record's groove. While the card plays,
  Space and the arrows belong to it (pause with the button, or once it is done).
* **What doing it by hand gives** — nothing is lost if you let her do it herself; done with
  its gesture, each item gets a ✋ in the app's checklist (hover it: what it added) and:

  | count the float | the till ×1.2 all night (not only 2 h) | the till ×1.05 more | the till ×1.05 all night |
  | the specials board | wishes for the specials' tastes ×1.2 tonight | ×1.1 more | ×1.1 |
  | put a record on | +2 coziness for an hour | +1 more | +1 for an hour |
  | the dishes | the dishes ×1.1 the next night's first hour | +0.5 coziness then | dirt −3 % |
  | clean the group head | the machine's wear −0.02 more | −0.01 more | −0.01 |
  | cash up | the till ×1.1 the next night's first hour | a small tip (10–20 Kč) | ×1.05 |

  **The evening mood**: each opening item done by hand is a point (0–7; a perfect gesture counts
  the same). Points made before 23:00 make the guests 4 % more patient and 3 % more generous
  each until 23:00 — a chip by the clock ("Mood 4/7") shows it from 21:30. Each closing item by
  hand puts +1 on the whole crew's mood when the next night starts. The morning summary says
  how many you did by hand and what it gave (the mood's tips in Kč).
  **Grandma's habits**: an item done by hand on 5 nights (in total, any nights) becomes one of
  Lída's habits — her note in her own words, and the perk above for good, whoever does the
  item. One a night at most (another one due comes the next time its item is done); the
  habits learned are listed under the app's checklists.
* **The tip jar** — tonight's tips and the lifetime total. When the jar reaches a goal, a
  "Count the jar" chip appears: pick one of two decor pieces for the hall.
* **Napkins** — a perfect cup for someone who needed it sometimes earns a folded thank-you
  napkin next to the tip coin. They are kept in the recipe book (the Napkins ribbon).

## Building (B)

* The build panel sits low at the bottom; the hall moves up (and zooms out a touch if it has
  to) so the counter and the corners stay visible above it. Holding a piece near the edge of
  the view pans towards it.
* **Cards** show the price and small chips for what a piece gives: ♥ beauty (coziness),
  seats, comfort, ☀ light reach, music, a work station, a socket. Hover a card for the
  description and the exact numbers. Locked cards say how they unlock ("From night 6",
  "When you invite Lori to play"). A face and *"Filip is waiting"* mark a piece a regular
  asked for (the tab shows an amber count too).
* **The fridge keeps the stock.** About 40 % of the supplies are fresh (milk, cream, the
  cake, the chlebíček) and live in the fridge; grandma's fridge holds about 1 100 Kč of them.
  Whatever is beyond that at 6 in the morning partly goes off — the morning summary says so
  (*"11 l of milk went off — the worn fridge holds less"*). A worn fridge holds less (defrost it
  when it sputters), the morning after it gave out it holds half, and the closing fridge check
  saves a little. A **second fridge** doubles the room (its card says *Room for fresh stock:
  1 100 → 2 200*) and the summary counts what it kept (*"Second fridge: nothing spoiled · saved
  450 Kč"*). The planning card shows the fridge now (*Fridge (fresh) 940 / 1 100 Kč*, amber
  when it's worn or over full), and so does the kitchen shelf in the pantry. With the usual
  restock and a fridge in good repair nothing goes off.
* **Moving**: press on a placed piece and drag it — a table takes its chairs along. **R**
  turns the whole group. It's free and **Ctrl+Z** puts it back. A piece somebody uses (a
  guest on the chair, a helper at the machine) says so and stays put.
* **While holding a piece** the atmosphere meter and a little label over the piece show how
  much coziness it would add or take (`+0,6`), the lamps' warm pools appear with the seats
  they light (a warm dot = lit, a violet ring = in the dark), and decor shows its 3-tile
  beauty reach.

## Seasons, themes and the journal

* The month moves from late autumn (wet leaves) to frost, Advent (grandma's garlands — hang
  them when her box appears on the counter — and a wreath) and the first snow.
* **Build → Theme** — decor themes for the hall (Grandma's original, Prague Art Nouveau,
  Station Retro, Nocturne 2039, Winter Lights). Unlocked themes are bought once and switched
  for free during the day. A locked theme says how it unlocks (e.g. *reputation 50 or
  3 000 Kč in tips*); hover it to see how far along you are.
* **The polaroid journal (J)** — every morning the night's best moment develops in the summary
  (drag over it to shake it faster); click one of the two small ones to keep that instead.
  The journal keeps one per night — thirty slots in the story.

## Karolina's own small life: the walk home and the back step

* **The walk home** — after the morning summary Karolina locks the café and walks home through
  dawn Holešovice (about 20 seconds: the sky goes from indigo to peach, the lamps go out,
  pigeons take off). While she locks up, a small row asks **Which way home?** — one click (or
  **1–4**); otherwise she takes her usual way (the last one you picked) after a few seconds.
  **Space / Enter / Esc** or *Skip* ends it at once. Each way gives the *next* night one small,
  cozy touch (hover a chip to see it), nothing more:
  * **Embankment** — along the Vltava (the bridge, houseboats, a swan): quick brews a touch
    better tonight (+0.05 quality).
  * **Stromovka** — through the park: a calm hall for the first 90 minutes and guests a little
    more patient for the first 2½ hours (×1.08).
  * **Bakery** — past the corner bakery opening at six: a kolač on the counter, coziness +3
    for the first four hours.
  * **Tram** — the first tram; you talk to a nurse after her night shift, and tonight she drops
    by (around 22:55, with a wish).
  The lines on the way change with the season (frost, Advent lights, the first snow with your
  footprints) and the weather (rain and an umbrella, fog). Skipping never costs anything.
* **A break on the back step** — at night, from 22:30 to 05:40 and not during a rush, a small
  chip **Step out back** sits beside the atmosphere meter, once a night. Karolina sits on the
  step with a cup: rain dripping off the awning (or snow, or stars), a night train behind the
  fence, the station cat comes over (closer the more it trusts you), and a message arrives on
  her phone — from mum, her friend Terka or Mrs Marta, grandma's old neighbour; the messages
  follow the story's acts. The café simply waits while you are out (nobody minds). No stats —
  just an exhale. *Back inside* / Esc returns; it ends by itself after half a minute.
* **What you hear** — keys in the lock, her steps on the wet pavement, the pigeons, the tram's
  bell and her front door on the way home; the back door's creak, rain on the awning (the storm's
  wind on a storm night), the phone buzzing and the cat's meow and purr (when it trusts her) on
  the back step. In the café each chore at an appliance has its own sound (the machine's hiss,
  the purge, the knock box, the pot lid and the simmering soup, the oven's ding, the fridge, the
  till's coins, the trash bag and the door, the lamp switch), and the recipe book rustles. These
  are CC0 recordings (credits in `assets/audio/CREDITS.md`) and play even in a source build;
  the Sound slider and **M** cover them.

## Little stories of strangers and the storm night

**Little stories.** Now and then a stranger (never a regular) carries a small story: a phone
face up waiting for a call, the last train to Děčín gone, a first pay cheque, a birthday
alone, a nurse after a hard shift, a student before the state exam, a couple not talking, a
cello case by the chair… A small **amber speech bubble with three dots** floats beside their
head (moment markers are indigo orbs on the other side; it stays readable zoomed out). Click
it: a small card shows what Karolina notices and two or three answers — a free slice of
cake, a warm word, the charger, the window seat, a taxi through the app, a record. One click
and she walks over (if they get up to leave first, the card and the bubble close and her
walk is called off — "They've already left"; the same for a moment about a guest). Every
answer is kind; the one that fits gets a warm reaction, a small
reward (the guest's mood, a tip in the jar, sometimes a thank-you napkin or a cozier hall), a
line in the night log and a polaroid candidate for the morning, and they tell her their name.
Ignoring a story is fine: it fades after a while or when they leave. At most two a night, one
at a time, never during a rush, never the same story twice in a game (22 of them).

**The storm night.** Once in the story — night 29, Mikuláš eve — a snowstorm closes the line
(in the sandbox, now and then on a snowy winter night). It snows hard from the start; at
00:50 the station announces that every night train is cancelled until 06:00 and
@shiftella_bot writes. From then on no train comes and no stranger walks in (regulars still
do — they live nearby), and everyone already inside stays till morning: at 06:00 the doors open
for them and they walk out into the snow, then the closing half hour runs as usual. A small **Snowed in**
card under the atmosphere meter says how many stay and offers two one-click chores:
**Blankets for all** (grandma's plaids from under the counter — you'll see everyone tucked in)
and **A pot of soup** (the big pot on the stove, a bowl for everyone). At 03:00 the stranded
strangers talk — a short scene with their names and faces (click / Space to go on, Esc skips).
In the morning the night's special polaroid waits, and if you looked after them the café's
reputation rises a little. Nothing here touches the story's ending.

## The recipe book (K)

Drinks, food and specials you know (with grandma's notes, how often you've made them and
your best cup), and silhouettes of the ones you don't — their tastes, how many ingredients,
and a hint where to learn them. **Pantry**: put 2–3 jars in the slots and **Try**. By day the
result is instant; at night Karolina tries it at the counter. "So close" lines tell you what
to swap; every few tries Lida's margin note reveals an ingredient on a silhouette.

## The café's own drink

From night 6 grandma's note in the drawer suggests it (a toast, once): every café worth its
name has a drink of its own. Open the recipe book (**K**) and press **Our drink** under it:

* **Base** — coffee, black tea, cocoa or linden tea (one click; a base whose jar isn't on the
  shelf yet is greyed out).
* **Add 2–3** — jars from the pantry that make sense in a cup (milk, honey, spice, lemon, ice,
  vanilla…; at most one milk). Only what you already have.
* **Made by** — the mini-game it is brewed with: pour-over, frothed, latte art or steeped
  (what suits the base).
* The **tastes** follow from what's in it (a little ice makes it a cold drink) and so does the
  price — the base's price plus a modest 20–25 Kč.
* **Name** — type up to 24 letters (Czech and Cyrillic letters work), or click one of the
  three **ideas** (↻ shows more: *Holešovická noc*, *Lídin čaj*, *Déšť na kolejích*…).
* **Chalk it on the board.** A little A-frame chalkboard appears by the left end of the
  counter with the name and the price (hover it for the tastes, click it to open the card).

From then on it is a recipe like any other: wishes score it by its tastes, and now and then
strangers and the regulars ask for it **by name** ("«Holešovická noc», please — the one on
the board"): it's their favourite, so it's always a perfect match. The night you made it you
can still tweak it; after that it can be reworked or renamed **once a week**. It's never
needed for the story.

## Photo mode (P)

At night press **P** or the small camera under the atmosphere meter (the amber number is the
film left tonight: 3 a night). The café holds still and a viewfinder frames part of it: move a
little with **WASD** / right-drag, zoom with the wheel, then **click** (or **Enter** / **Space**)
to take the photo. A soft flash, the print slides into the corner and develops — drag over it
to shake it faster; click it to open the journal. **Esc** (or **P**) puts the camera away
without a photo. The photos go into the journal as extra photos of that night: small prints
tucked beside the night's polaroid; open the night and step through them with **← →**. They
never replace the polaroid you keep in the morning.

## Pause on important events

**Settings → General → Pause on important events** (off by default; the sandbox setup has the
same box, and a sandbox café keeps it in its save — either one turns it on). When it is on, the
night pauses itself — the same pause as **Space** — when something needs you, and a small chip
under the top bar says why, with its icon: *Paused: Thunderstorm*. **Space** or the chip's
**Continue** plays on at the speed you had.

What pauses: a bad event starting (the storm, a power cut, the fridge, a student party… — the
serious ones; small bad events only when they ask for an answer), a regular walking in with a
new part of their story to tell, a stranger with a little story, a helper falling ill or walking
out, a phone message that needs an answer (a swap or time-off request, a late or sick call, low
stock), the café running out of supplies, and on **Classic** a guest about to give up and leave
unhappy.

It never nags: at most one auto-pause per 15 minutes of the night, never by day, and never over a
story scene, a story card you are reading, a mini-game, a ritual gesture, the walk home or photo
mode — those already hold the night. Something that happens meanwhile pauses right after the
mini-game / gesture / menu closes (within a few minutes), not after a scene or a story card.
If you paused yourself, nothing changes.

## Accessibility

**Settings → Accessibility**:

* **Text size** — 100 / 115 / 130 %. Only the text grows (titles, labels, captions), not the UI as
  a whole — that's the **UI scale** on the General tab; the two combine. Panels that are sized by
  their text grow with it; fixed widgets shrink a long word a little before it would be cut.
* **Colour-blind friendly markers** (on by default) — everything over the café that used to differ
  only by its colour gets a shape as well: a regular's ticket on the rail gets a small **star**
  (not only the amber ring), an open opening/closing ritual over the furniture is an **empty box**
  to tick (not a tick, which reads as done), tip coins get a light **rim** so they never melt into
  the warm floor, and a dirt blot gets a dashed **"mop here" ring**. The order marks were already
  shapes (**!** waiting, a **tick** when Karolina is on it, a cup with a heart for the usual, a
  glass of water), as are the wish bubble (a thought bubble with taste icons), the emotes and the
  night moments (an icon per kind). Off brings back the plain look.
* **Relaxed mini-games** — wider sweet spots, a quality floor and a Done button.

**No money pressure — the Carefree difficulty.** New story offers three presets: **Cozy**
(recommended), **Carefree** and **Classic**. Carefree plays exactly like Cozy, except that grandma's
debt never presses: each instalment is covered on its night (the debt shrinks, the till keeps its
money, a line in the log says so), nothing can be missed, and the story reaches «Light in the
Window» as on Cozy. The day card says "covered" next to the next instalment.

## The crew panel (C) — the character card

Click Karolina or a helper in the café (or press **C**) to open their card on the left. The
strip of faces on top is the whole crew — Karolina first, then the helpers (a green dot: on
the floor, amber: still to come, red: sick) and **Hire** with the number of candidates.
**Arrows** step through it, **Esc** closes the card.

* **Karolina's card** is about you: what she is doing **now** and what is queued **next**
  (× drops an act, like a right-click on the chip over her head), what **waits** only for
  her (orders, wishes, rounds to make, trays, tables, the sink, mud, tip coins, moments —
  hover a chip for what it is), **tonight's** numbers (made by hand, perfect cups, tips,
  rhythm / best) and the duties no helper holds — those are **yours** (delegate them on the
  Duties board, **H**). Energy shows only on Classic; on Cozy she never tires.
* **A helper's card**: role and age, tonight's shift (a pill: "22:00–02:00", "From 02:00",
  "Off tonight"…), what they do **now** and **next** in words ("Making: Latte · Mrs Vera",
  "Break around 01:30", "Home at 02:00"), their **duties** as six one-click toggles (the same
  as the Duties board — both always agree; the pips are their skill, a heart their
  favourite, the dots how many duties they can hold), **mood** with the reason ("Friction
  with Zdeněk", "Wish not kept: Sun off", "Happy with this week's schedule"), **energy**
  with the reason on Classic (on Cozy only when they get tired), traits (hover for what they
  do), their **wishes** for the schedule and who they get along with.
* Quick actions, one click, no questions: **Follow** (the camera keeps on them; a chip at the
  top says "Following Karolina · Esc / click to stop" — Esc, the chip, moving the camera or
  the card's **Stop following** ends it), **Break**
  (Classic, or when they're tired), **Send home** (ends their shift now; tired people are
  glad of it), **Schedule** (opens their row in the Shiftella rozpis), by day **Let go**.
* **Hire**: every candidate with their role, wage and what they are good at in one line.

The panel is only as tall as the card; on a small screen or at UI scale 1.3 the card scrolls.

## Guests and regulars

Hover a guest to see a little bubble: who they are and what they're waiting for. Regulars
say something about the night instead. Click a regular to pin the bubble — when they have
something to tell you tonight, a **Talk** button appears.

Mirek, Hana, Vera, Filip, Radek, Lori and the quiet guest of 3:33 each have a small story
over the month. Serve them well and in time, keep their seat free, keep the place clean.

**Strangers** are everyone else — travellers, nurses, cabbies, students… You don't know
them yet, so they are shown by what you see: "Woman in a flat cap", "Student in headphones",
"Elderly gentleman in a hat" (the ticket rail shows a 1–2 word form, hover a ticket for the
whole one). Wave at them when they come in, or serve them a cup made by your own hands, and
they tell you their name ("I'm Tereza, thank you!"): from then on the bubble, the rail, the
order card and the night log (L) use it. A group reads "Tereza + 2". Strangers don't come
back on another night — tomorrow brings new faces. Regulars are always known by name.

## The Shiftella app (T)

Everything in the app does something tonight or tomorrow. While the phone is open at night,
the clock crawls.

* **Rozpis** — a light weekly puzzle. Every helper has a few preference chips ("Sun off",
  "Late shifts", "Likes working with Bára"; hover for their own words; a firmer border is a
  Need). Cells cycle Full → Early → Late → off (right-click goes back, drag paints). Chips glow
  emerald when honoured and amber when the draft breaks them; the ring on the right is their
  wellbeing, the badge below is the week's weather. **Auto-fill** keeps as many wishes as it
  can and says which didn't fit; **Publish** sends it. A happy week shows in the crew's mood.
  Your own row is locked: you're there every night.
* **Requests** — swaps, time off (dovolená, náhradní volno) and "Can I go at 2?": answering
  matters for people's wellbeing.
* **Messages** — @shiftella_bot. The buttons act in the café (reserve a regular's seat,
  remind me before Radek's train, ask someone to cover, order by drone…). A button that can't
  act right now says why and the message stays open. (The bot never does clock-ins.)
* **Board** — announcements; at closing (from 05:00, and in the closing half hour) pick up to two handover notes for
  tomorrow — each one does something next night.
* **Checklists** — the opening and closing rituals, with whose job each item is and who is on
  it right now. The opening list fits in the preparation half hour (21:30–22:00) before the
  first guests, the closing list in the closing half hour (06:00–06:30).
  Every item reads the same here and on its box in the café: an **empty box** = open (a click
  sends Karolina); a **clock** = she has it queued / is on her way ("Karolina · on her way" —
  a second click cancels it); a **person** = a helper is on it; a **tick**, struck through, with
  who and when = done. A click never looks like *done* before it is.
* **Night digest** — last night's numbers and up to four insights, each with a link
  (Rozpis, Recipe book, Pantry, Build, Duties…).
* **Payroll basis** (podklady pro mzdy) — the week's hours and wages, the tip split (all to
  the café / half / all to the crew, with the real numbers), a star of the week, owed
  náhradní volno → a night off, and **Send to the accountant**.

## Saving

* Autosave: at the start of every day and when you quit. A save made in the preparation
  half hour loads there (doors still closed), one in the closing half hour there too; 0.1.0
  and 0.2.0 saves load as before (a 0.2.0 night goes on into the new closing half hour).
* Three manual slots in the pause menu (**Esc** → Save game / Load game).
* **Continue** in the main menu loads the most recent save.
* Files: `~/Library/Application Support/Shiftella/saves/` (fallback `./saves/`).

## The crash log

* `shiftella.log` lives in the save folder (`~/Library/Application Support/Shiftella/saves/`
  on macOS, `%APPDATA%\Shiftella\saves\` on Windows, `~/.config/shiftella/saves/`
  elsewhere; `SHIFTELLA_SAVES` moves it with the saves). **Settings → Log folder**
  opens it (macOS `open`, Windows Explorer, `xdg-open`); the button's tooltip shows the path.
* It is plain text and **never sent anywhere** — attach it to a bug report if you like.
* It keeps the last 5 runs and at most 64 KB (older runs drop off at the next start):
  each run starts with a header (version, time in UTC, OS + version + CPU), then a few info
  lines — startup, the language, scene changes (menu, day N, preparation, night N, a
  cutscene, credits), save / load / settings errors, quit. Nothing per frame; a run stops
  after 400 lines.
* If the game crashes, the panic is written with its message, source location, thread,
  version, OS, language, scene, the night's clock and a short backtrace:

## Tips

* The first two nights are yours alone; the first helper can start on night 3 (crew panel).
* Give each helper one clear duty first (dishes is a good start) — the guests stay yours.
* The night bar runs 21:30 → 06:30: the faint stretches at both ends are the preparation and
  the closing half hour (doors closed), the white ticks are 22:00 and 06:00.
* Trains bring small rushes (the markers on the night progress bar). Quick-brew the plain
  tickets and keep the wishes for by hand. On Cozy, when your hands are full, the next
  passengers (and walk-ins) wait outside a little and then come in one party at a time — never
  a wall of tickets; Classic keeps its rushes.
* Pendant lamps, plants and a rug make the hall nicer; guests stay happier.
* A paused game is a fine place to think. Event buttons still work while paused.
* On Cozy nothing can really go wrong. Classic makes the payments matter.

## Reasons to build: wishes, corners, the attic, the back room

* **More seats, more guests.** Every table, sofa or window seat you add draws more passers-by
  in (the build card says about how many); the bar stools by the counter only catch the
  overflow. Comfy pieces keep guests longer and tip better.
* **Wishes for the café.** Regulars, helpers and guests sometimes say what they'd like —
  Filip needs a table with a socket, Hana a quiet corner, Vera a seat by the window, Mirek
  grandma's old radio, the crew a coat rack… They say it once in a bubble, and every wish
  sits as a card in the Shiftella app (T → Requests, "Wishes for the café"): *Waiting*,
  *Built — they'll see it*, *Thanked*. "Build" on a card opens build mode. When the piece
  stands and the person is in the café, they thank you (a bubble + a toast) and there is a
  small reward: trust, a napkin, a better mood, a happier table. Story wishes (Filip, Lori,
  Hana) are marked "Matters to their story" — their arcs still need the same piece as before;
  the card only tells you. Nothing here is needed to finish the story.
* **Corners.** Pieces placed close together in the hall form a named corner: a *reading
  nook* (armchair + lamp + bookshelf), a *window spot* (window seat + plant + lamp), a
  *little stage* (stage + lamp), a *listening corner* (jukebox or radio + a soft seat), a
  *coat corner*, a *study table* (socket table + lamp), a *green corner* (three plants). Each
  adds a little coziness (up to +8 together) and draws its kind of guests a bit more often.
  A toast says when one forms.
* **Grandma's attic** (day card → *Grandma's attic*). From night 3, and every Saturday after,
  three second-hand pieces come down from the attic at a flea-market price — each with a
  line about grandma Lida (her sewing chair, the waiting-room bench Jindra sat on, the tram
  lamp she traded for three cakes…). One click on *Bring down* buys it and puts it in the
  hall; drag it where you like in build mode. Removed again, it goes back upstairs and comes
  down for free. Each piece exists once; things come down in the daytime.
* **The back room** (in the same panel). From night 15 grandma's back room can be reopened
  for 14 000 Kč: the café grows by a small second hall behind the right wall, with two of
  her tables (4 seats) and floor to decorate. A Cozy player usually has the money by night
  16–20. It's optional.
* **Before / after.** When the hall changed noticeably during the day (a new corner, the
  back room, clearly cosier), the next morning's polaroids include a *before / after* print:
  the same spot last night and tonight, side by side.

## For developers: automation runs

Screenshot, autoplay and QA runs (`SHIFTELLA_SHOT`, `SHIFTELLA_AUTOPLAY`, or
`SHIFTELLA_HIDDEN=1`) are **invisible** on macOS: no Dock icon, never frontmost, no focus
taken, no sound, the window fully transparent and click-through behind the other windows —
the game still renders every frame and the screenshot is the real picture (`src/platform.rs`).
`SHIFTELLA_HIDDEN=0` keeps the window visible, e.g. to watch an autoplay run. Prefer the
headless checks (`cargo test`, `game_autoplay`, `sim_balance`, `sim_headless`) where a
picture isn't needed.

Package C scenes: `SHIFTELLA_SCENE=attic` (day 13, the attic panel), `backroom` (night 16,
00:30, the reopened back room), `requests` (night 12, the app's Requests tab), `corner`
(day 9, a reading nook just placed, build mode), `before_after` (morning after night 9 with
the before / after polaroid picked); `SHIFTELLA_NIGHT` overrides the night.
Character card scenes: `SHIFTELLA_SCENE=crew_karolina|crew_helper|crew_classic|crew_day|crew_hire`
(story night 8 at 00:30 unless `SHIFTELLA_NIGHT` / `SHIFTELLA_TIME` say otherwise;
`crew_classic` plays the story on Classic, `crew_day` opens the card in the day phase).
`SHIFTELLA_TEST_PANIC=1` makes the game panic on purpose right after boot, to check the crash
log (`src/crashlog.rs`) by hand; nothing is saved. `cargo test --test crashlog` checks it
end to end (rolling runs, no per-frame lines, a caught panic).

Package I scenes: `SHIFTELLA_SCENE=signature_compose` (day 7, the house drink's card half
filled), `signature_board` (night 8, 00:30, the chalkboard by the counter), `photo_mode`
(night 8, the viewfinder up), `photo_print` (a print developing in the corner;
`SHIFTELLA_DEVELOP=0..1`), `journal_photos` (day 9, extra photos on nights 3, 5 and 7;
`SHIFTELLA_OPEN=<slot>` enlarges a night, `SHIFTELLA_SHOWN=<n>` picks its photo).
`SHIFTELLA_SIG_NAME` names the drink; `SHIFTELLA_NIGHT` / `SHIFTELLA_TIME` as usual.

`SHIFTELLA_TIME=HH:MM` also takes the two quiet halves: `21:30`–`21:59` start the night
before the doors open (e.g. `SHIFTELLA_SCENE=night SHIFTELLA_NIGHT=1 SHIFTELLA_TIME=21:40`),
`06:00`–`06:29` the closing half hour after the doors closed (`06:29`: the waiting clock).
`SHIFTELLA_DOORS=wait` leaves the opening / closing list to the crew, so `21:59` / `06:29` show
the clock waiting for the doors / for "Close up".

`SHIFTELLA_SCENE=hands_ux SHIFTELLA_HUX=gesture SHIFTELLA_GESTURE=<checklist id | equip.<chore>>`
shows a ritual gesture over its piece (default night 1 at 21:40, `light_window`; use
`SHIFTELLA_DOORS=wait` so the list is still open, `SHIFTELLA_TIME=06:05` for a closing item);
`SHIFTELLA_GESTURE_T=<s>` plays it by script that long first (never to the end). A fresh
`SHIFTELLA_SETTINGS` file shows the first-time hint.

`SHIFTELLA_BYHAND=all|opening|closing|<id>[:p],…` on `night` / `summary` / `hands_ux` marks
those checklist items done by hand tonight (`:p` = perfect; `all` = every other one perfect);
`SHIFTELLA_HABITS=4` counts every item done by hand on 4 nights before (so tonight learns a
habit), `SHIFTELLA_MOOD_TIPS=<Kč>` the evening mood's tips for the summary,
`SHIFTELLA_HABITS_SCROLL=1` scrolls the app's checklists to grandma's habits and
`SHIFTELLA_HABIT_CARD=<id>` opens that habit's card (e.g. `SHIFTELLA_SCENE=night
SHIFTELLA_TIME=21:45 SHIFTELLA_BYHAND=light_window:p,record_on` for the mood chip).

`SHIFTELLA_FRIDGE=full|worn` on `SHIFTELLA_SCENE=day|summary` puts 4 200 Kč in stock (and
wears grandma's fridge to 0.5 with `worn`): the planning card's fridge row turns amber and
the morning summary shows what went off.

`SHIFTELLA_SCENE=equipment SHIFTELLA_EQUIP=prep|night|worn|closing` plays the appliances to a
picture: the preparation (soup on, milk out, the cake baking, the machine warming up), the
night (the pot bubbling, the oven's ding, a tray of shots), a worn machine being purged, and
closing (the till counted, the trash run). `SHIFTELLA_ZOOM=2` follows Karolina closer.
`SHIFTELLA_SCENE=walk_home|walk_home_rain|walk_home_snow|backstep|backstep_snow` shows the walk
home (after the night's summary) or the back step; `SHIFTELLA_ROUTE=embankment|park|bakery|tram`
picks the way, `SHIFTELLA_T=<seconds>` jumps into the scene, `SHIFTELLA_WEATHER=rain|downpour|fog|snow|clear`
sets the sky, `SHIFTELLA_NIGHT` the season.
`SHIFTELLA_SCENE=story_marker|story_card` plays story night 8 (or `SHIFTELLA_NIGHT`) to
`SHIFTELLA_TIME` (default 23:00) until a stranger carries a story (if none came within 30 min,
`SHIFTELLA_STORY=<id>` — default `call` — is put on a seated stranger) and shows the marker /
its card. `SHIFTELLA_SCENE=storm` plays night 29 to 01:20 with the blankets and the soup asked
for (the Snowed-in card, the toasts); `storm_talk` to the 03:00 talk, `SHIFTELLA_LINE=n` its
line (0 = the narration).
`SHIFTELLA_SCENE=auto_pause` plays story night 3 (or `SHIFTELLA_NIGHT`) to `SHIFTELLA_TIME`
(default 00:30), starts `SHIFTELLA_EVENT=<id>` (default `storm`) and shows the night auto-paused
for it with its reason chip (automation never pauses by itself).
