# Playing Shiftella

You inherit grandma Lida's night café, Café Nocturne, next to Praha-Holešovice station in Prague.
It opens at 22:00 and closes at 06:00. Keep the light in the window on, keep the regulars
warm, pay off the debt — or let the café go, gently. Nothing here is meant to stress you.

## The rhythm

1. **Day (planning).** The clock waits. On the right is the day card: tonight's story hint,
   money, the next debt payment, who may drop by, train times, how many people are on shift.
   Hire people (**C**), arrange the rozpis in the Shiftella app (**T**), build (**B**).
   Press **Start the night** (or **Enter**).
2. **Night.** Guests arrive on foot and from the trains; your crew takes orders, makes coffee
   and food, serves, clears tables, washes up and mops — by their work priorities (**P**).
   Events pop up as toasts; some have buttons (they wait while paused, and fall back to a
   default after a while).
3. **Morning.** A short summary of the night: guests, revenue, tips, wages, costs, reputation,
   grandma's notes found. Then the next day begins (and the game autosaves).

Story mode has 30 nights and exactly two endings — «Light in the Window» and «Last Train».
Sandbox is endless, with a random town from a seed you can share.

## Controls

| Key | |
|---|---|
| **WASD / arrows**, right-drag, wheel | move / zoom the camera · **Home** — whole café |
| **Space** | pause / resume |
| **1 / 2 / 3** | speed ×1 / ×2 / ×3 (also the buttons on the clock card) |
| **C** | crew — needs, skills, traits, relationships, what they're doing; hire, let go |
| **P** | work priorities — click a cell to raise, right-click to lower, wheel works too |
| **T** | the Shiftella app — rozpis, requests, messages, board, checklists, digest, payroll |
| **B** | build — click a piece, click in the café; **R** rotate, **X** demolish, **Ctrl+Z** undo, right-click / **Esc** cancel |
| **L** | log (tonight / story) — also a click on the ticker at the top |
| **M** | sound on / off |
| **Esc** | close the open thing; with nothing open — the pause menu (settings, save, load, main menu, quit) |
| **Enter** | start the night (day) · close the morning summary |

In cutscenes: click / **Space** — next line, **1–4** — choices, hold **Esc** — skip,
**A** — auto-advance, hold **Ctrl** — faster.

## Guests and regulars

Hover a guest to see a little bubble: who they are and what they're waiting for. Regulars
say something about the night instead. Click a regular to pin the bubble — when they have
something to tell you tonight, a **Talk** button appears.

Mirek, Hana, Vera, Filip, Radek, Lori and the quiet guest of 3:33 each have a small story
over the month. Serve them well and in time, keep their seat free, keep the place clean.

## The Shiftella app (T)

* **Rozpis** — the week's shifts per person (22–06, 22–02, 02–06, off). Click a cell to
  change the **draft**, right-click to go back; **Publish** sends it to everyone. The night
  uses the published rozpis; last-minute edits make the crew a little grumpy.
* **Requests** — shift swaps and time off (dovolená, náhradní volno): approve or decline.
* **Messages** — @shiftella_bot: late-arrival alerts, requests, the night digest. Some have
  buttons. (The bot never does clock-ins — people aren't punch cards.)
* **Board** — announcements and the předávka (handover note) for the next shift.
* **Checklists** — opening and closing items; some are yours (light the window, the handover
  note), the crew does the rest when there's time.
* **Night digest** and **Payroll basis** (podklady pro mzdy) — hours, night hours and wages
  per person, ready for the accountant.

## Saving

* Autosave: at the start of every day and when you quit.
* Three manual slots in the pause menu (**Esc** → Save game / Load game).
* **Continue** in the main menu loads the most recent save.
* Files: `~/Library/Application Support/Shiftella/saves/` (fallback `./saves/`).

## Tips

* The first nights are quiet. Hire one or two people early — the candidates are in the crew panel.
* Set priorities so everyone has one clear "1" job; nobody likes doing everything.
* Trains bring small rushes (the markers on the night progress bar). Have someone on the register.
* Pendant lamps, plants and a rug make the hall nicer; guests stay happier.
* A paused game is a fine place to think. Event buttons still work while paused.
* On Cozy nothing can really go wrong. Classic makes the payments matter.
